# Lisbon Travel Guide

## Best time to visit
- Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–October): warm, sunny, comfortable for walking.
- Summer (June–August) is hot and busy, with festivals in June (Santo António).
- Winter is mild but rainy; good for lower prices.

## Layout and neighbourhoods
- Lisbon is built on hills. Expect steep streets and polished cobblestones; wear comfortable shoes with grip.
- Baixa / Chiado: flat central area, shopping, easy access to transport.
- Alfama: oldest district, narrow lanes, fado music, viewpoints; hilly and best explored on foot.
- Bairro Alto: restaurants and nightlife; can be noisy at night.
- Belém: monuments and museums, a short tram or train ride west of the centre.

## Getting around
- The Metro is simple and covers the main areas; Lisbon Airport (LIS) is directly connected by the red line.
- Trams (such as line 28) are scenic but crowded; ride early or late to avoid queues and pickpockets.
- A rechargeable transport card (Viva Viagem) is cheaper than single tickets.
- Ride-hailing and taxis are inexpensive compared with many European capitals.
- Funiculars and elevators (Santa Justa, Glória, Bica) help with the hills.

## Must-see sights
- Belém Tower and Jerónimos Monastery (buy tickets ahead for the monastery; closed on Mondays).
- São Jorge Castle for city views.
- Miradouros (viewpoints): Santa Luzia, Senhora do Monte, São Pedro de Alcântara.
- Day trip to Sintra (palaces; start early) and Cascais by train.

## Food tips
- Pastéis de nata (custard tarts), bacalhau (salted cod), grilled sardines in summer, and petiscos (small plates).
- Many restaurants bring bread, olives and cheese to the table; these are charged only if you eat them.
- Lunch specials (prato do dia) are good value.

## Practical tips
- Watch for pickpockets on tram 28 and in busy tourist areas.
- Many museums close on Mondays.
- Distances in the centre are short, but allow extra time for hills.

## Sample 2-day pacing
- Day 1: Baixa and Chiado, Alfama and São Jorge Castle, fado dinner.
- Day 2: Belém in the morning, then a viewpoint sunset; or take a Sintra day trip instead.
