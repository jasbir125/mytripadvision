# Travel Safety, Health and Money Basics

## Before you go
- Check that your passport is valid for at least 6 months beyond your travel dates; many countries require this.
- Keep digital copies of your passport, visa, tickets and insurance in cloud storage and a separate printed copy.
- Buy travel insurance that covers medical care, trip cancellation and lost baggage.
- Tell your bank your travel plans and carry at least two payment methods.
- Check government travel advisories for your destination close to departure.

## Health
- Review vaccination recommendations for your destination with a doctor or travel clinic several weeks before travel.
- Carry prescription medicines in their original packaging with a doctor's note.
- Pack a small kit: pain relief, oral rehydration salts, antiseptic, plasters, any personal medicines.
- In hot climates, drink bottled or treated water if tap water is not known to be safe.

## Staying safe
- Keep valuables in a front pocket or zipped cross-body bag in crowded areas.
- Use the hotel safe for passports and spare cash.
- Use licensed taxis or trusted ride-hailing apps; confirm the fare or use the meter.
- Be cautious with strangers offering unsolicited help, tours or currency exchange.
- Share your itinerary with someone at home.

## Money
- Use ATMs inside banks or malls rather than isolated machines.
- When paying by card abroad, choose to be charged in the local currency to avoid poor conversion rates.
- Keep a small amount of local cash for markets, tips and small shops.
- Check the local tipping custom; it varies widely by country.

## Packing essentials
- Universal power adapter and a portable charger.
- Comfortable walking shoes and weather-appropriate layers.
- Reusable water bottle, sunscreen, and a basic first-aid kit.
- Offline maps downloaded before you arrive.
