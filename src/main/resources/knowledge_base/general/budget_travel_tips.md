# Budget Travel Tips

## How to split a trip budget (rough guide)
- Flights: often 30–45% of a short international trip.
- Accommodation: 25–35%.
- Food: 10–20%.
- Local transport and attractions: 10–15%.
- Keep 5–10% as a buffer for surprises.
- For short trips of 2–3 days, flights and hotels dominate; for longer stays, daily costs matter more.

## Saving on flights
- Be flexible with dates; midweek flights are often cheaper than weekends.
- Compare nearby airports and consider one-stop itineraries.
- Book international flights roughly 6–10 weeks ahead and domestic flights 3–6 weeks ahead as a general rule of thumb; set price alerts.
- Carry-on only avoids baggage fees on low-cost airlines; check bag allowances before booking.

## Saving on stays
- Staying a little outside the centre near a metro or train station can cut costs significantly.
- Compare hotels, guesthouses and apartments; apartments with a kitchen help on longer stays.
- Check cancellation policies and what is included, such as breakfast, taxes and city fees.

## Saving on food
- Eat the main meal at lunch; set lunch menus are often cheaper than dinner.
- Use markets, bakeries and street food for quick, cheap meals.
- Avoid restaurants right next to major sights, where prices are usually higher.

## Saving on getting around and sights
- Use public transport day passes or city cards only if you will use enough rides or attractions to make them worthwhile.
- Walk where possible.
- Many cities have free walking tours, free museum days or evening discounts; check the official tourism site.

## Currency and payments
- Avoid exchanging money at airport counters; use ATMs or card payments at a fair rate.
- Keep track of daily spending against the budget every evening.
